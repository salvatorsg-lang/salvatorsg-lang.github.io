How I use AI in my CS work

AI is my drafting and thinking partner — never a replacement for judgement, and never customer-facing on its own. Before a QBR I feed it account notes and usage numbers for an outline; on health reviews I score accounts with a simple method and let a script rank them so I spend time on the at-risk ones; on messy situations — churn risk, escalations, rough call notes — it turns the mess into a structured first draft, and I add the context only a human on the account knows.

The full working toolkit — prompts, templates, a worked example, and the health-score script — is on GitHub: github.com/salvatorsg-lang/cs-toolkit.
