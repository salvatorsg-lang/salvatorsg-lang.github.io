AI prompt playground

Three prompts I use with an AI assistant for CS work. Pick a scenario to see the prompt and a sample output. Sample data — the fictional Northwind Media example from my toolkit.

The prompt

Act as a customer success manager preparing a quarterly business review.

Account: [name]
Quarter: [Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 + year]
Account notes: [paste]
Usage numbers: [paste — spend, adoption, active campaigns/users]

Give me a QBR outline with:
1. Top 3 wins this quarter, each with the number that proves it
2. Adoption — what's being used, what isn't, and why it matters
3. Top 3 risks, each with one concrete next step
4. Expansion talking points — where the customer could get more value
5. Three questions to ask the customer in the room

Keep it to one page. Flag anything you can't verify from what I pasted.

Sample output — Northwind Media (fictional)

Wins: campaign reporting time cut from 2 days to 4 hours for the two teams using it; two new client teams onboarded; zero downtime incidents.

Adoption: 61% seat usage; the reporting module is the gap — worth a live demo in the QBR once the export issue is fixed.

Risks: reporting exports failing; champion left, replacement not onboarded; sponsor hasn't attended in 2 months — each with a named owner and date.

Expansion: the third client team is still on manual reporting — the natural next step, but only after the export issue is resolved. Don't pitch expansion on top of a broken feature.

The prompt

Help me triage a churn risk and draft a save plan.

Account: [name]
Usage trend: [e.g. active users down 40% in 6 weeks]
Ticket themes: [paste]
Last meaningful contact: [date + who]
Contract ends: [date]

Give me:
1. The most likely reason they're disengaging, in one paragraph
2. A save plan as a table: action | owner | date | what "done" looks like
3. What to say in the first outreach message — 3 sentences, human tone, no jargon
4. One thing I should NOT do yet

Be direct. Don't soften bad news.

Sample output — Northwind Media (fictional)

Most likely reason: active users fell from 16 to 11 in 6 weeks because reporting exports keep failing — and the champion who would have flagged it left 5 weeks ago, so nobody inside is pushing usage.

Save plan: fix the reporting export bug or put a workaround in place (Support + me, this week); onboard the new champion with a 30-minute intro and a quick win (me, next week); re-engage the sponsor with a one-page value summary before the QBR (me, before QBR).

First outreach: "Hi — I noticed reporting exports have been failing for your team, and I know that's slowing down your client reporting. I've got our team on it this week, and I'd like 30 minutes with you and your new lead to walk through a fix and a quick win. Does Thursday work?"

Don't do yet: don't pitch expansion to the third team until the export issue is resolved.

The prompt

Turn this messy ticket thread into a one-paragraph executive summary.

Ticket thread: [paste]
Impact: [who is affected, how badly]
Current status: [where things stand]

Give me:
1. One paragraph: what happened, impact, status, next step — plain language, no blame
2. A timeline: date + one line per key event
3. The one thing leadership should know that isn't obvious from the thread

Sample output — Northwind Media (fictional)

Summary: reporting exports have failed 5 times in the last 90 days for Northwind Media's 18-person team, which is why active users dropped from 16 to 11. Support has a workaround in testing and a fix targeted for this week; the next step is confirming the fix with the two affected teams and re-running their client reports.

What leadership should know: the tickets look like one issue, but the real risk is the champion who left 5 weeks ago — the replacement hasn't been onboarded, so there's nobody inside pushing usage while this is broken.
